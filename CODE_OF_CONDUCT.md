# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. We're here to build something useful, not to score points off each other.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings, not attacks.
- Keep criticism about the code, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and beginner questions.

## What's not okay

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, or publishing someone's
private information. Whether it happens in an issue, a pull request, or anywhere else tied to
this project, it's not welcome.

## Reporting

If someone crosses the line, email **conduct@videogpt.dev**. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and, for repeated or serious behavior, block people
from the project.
